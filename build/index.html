Modern Jeff

Just a Demo family trying to keep up with the modernization process.

Summary
  • This site covers everything up until the 2025 bond passed.
  • Voters approved the 2025 Bond. (Source)
  • Modernization Team answers FAQs. (Source)
  • Superintendent Armstrong reaffirmed commitment to Jefferson after April 9 meeting. (Source)
  • Jefferson has $366M for modernization from 2020 Bond; 2025 Bond adds more funds. (Source)
  • The Office of School Modernization in Cost Reduction Phase using School Board’s framework. (Source)
Background
  • 2025, April 8: New estimates from the Cost Reduction Phase came in at $458-466M.
  • 2025, February 11: PPS adopted Resolution 7051 to build high-quality high schools more cost-efficiently.
  • 2024, March 20: To keep students on site, PPS pivoted to demolishing and rebuilding Jeff. Estimated costs increased to $491M.
  • 2023, December 12: PPS adopted Resolution 6806, authorizing reassessment of the modernization design in order to keep Jefferson High School students and staff on-site during construction.
  • 2022, December 13: After a year of planning, costs rose to $366M. PPS approved Resolution 6627, authorizing the Comprehensive Plan.
  • 2020, July 28: PPS approved a bond proposal that allocated $311M for the modernization of Jeff.
